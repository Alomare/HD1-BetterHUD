# Better HUD

More information on the HUD of HELLDIVERS (2015), for the [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher) mod manager.

Player cards show the rounds left in the magazine and the grenade count, with health and stamina bars underneath. Enemies get health bars, and hits show damage numbers where they land. A reload circle and an ammo circle sit beside the Helldiver. Enemy bars show burns, poison and stuns, and stay at the edge of the screen while the enemy is out of view.

## How to use

Every part can be switched on or off in the Better HUD options in Obsidian, or in game from the pause menu under **Mods → Better HUD**. Changes in one place show in the other.

## Known issues

- Players on other computers get no stamina bar. The game keeps stamina on each computer and never sends it.
- Lasers and arc weapons heat or charge up instead of using rounds, so they get no round counter. The ammo circle shows their heat or charge.

Mod authors can add meters, flashes and status icons to the HUD through Better HUD: see [docs/api.md](docs/api.md).
