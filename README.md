# Better HUD

A mod for HELLDIVERS (2015) that adds to the game's HUD, for [Obsidian Mod Manager](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianModManager). Each part is an option in Obsidian, in the mod's details, and a row in the pause menu, under **Mods → Better HUD**. A change in one place shows in the other.

- **Ammo counter** (on): over the magazine count, the rounds left in the magazine, for guns, flamethrowers and launchers. Lasers and arc weapons, which heat or charge up instead, get no counter. The counter hides while a stratagem code is entered or a stratagem beacon is held.
- **Grenade counter** (on): the lower of the two grenade icons on each player card is replaced by the number of grenades, such as x3, so counts above two show. The upper icon stays white from one grenade up, and both the icon and the counter turn grey at none.
- **Player health bars** (on): a red bar under each player card, in missions. White chunks fade out where health was lost, as on the game's bars. A yellow bar over the top half shows the shield from the Shield Generator Pack or the shield upgrade in Helldivers Arcade, in the color of the game's own shield bar. Both fills get a soft shadow that is darkest at the left and clears toward the right. While a player is downed, the bar turns crimson as they bleed out, with a yellow bar under it showing how close they are to getting back up. After 10 seconds at full health, with any shield full or gone, the bar fades to grey at half opacity, and it comes back when health is lost.
- **Health numbers** (off): current and maximum health at the right of each player health bar.
- **Enemy health bars** (on): a bar over every enemy the player hits, and every heavy any player hurts. Bug bars are orange, cyborg bars red and Illuminate bars purple, slightly see-through. A white stretch shows the health lost since the hits began: every hit renews it, and it fades out all at once half a second after the last. A pulsing outline shows a burn (orange), poison (green) or stun (blue), bigger enemies get bigger bars, and a bar flashes white when the enemy dies. The bar of an enemy out of view waits at the edge of the screen, a small gap in, on the side the enemy is; with Third Person Camera on, the bar is hidden until the enemy is looked at. Bars follow the UI scale setting.
- **Reload circle** (on): a light grey ring to the left of each local Helldiver, a little over the chest, that fills while the weapon in hand reloads, on a see-through dark track. A finished reload makes the ring grow and fade out in a tenth of a second. The ring follows the UI scale setting.
- **Ammo circle** (on): a ring in the same spot. For weapons that fire rounds, a yellow ring filled with the share of the magazine left, fading to red as the magazine runs from 30% to empty. Lasers fill the ring counter-clockwise with heat, pulsing between yellow and red from half heat, faster as it heats, and arc weapons fill it in blue with the charge while the trigger is held: dim blue until the charge is enough to fire, bright blue from there, and when the charge is let go the ring shakes, turns white and fades out. Empty, the ring shakes and empties, the dark track flashes between black and dark red, and the reload button shows in the middle. An overheated laser does the same after a white flash, with a stronger red. The ring shows from the first shot and fades out over a second once 8 seconds pass without one. While the weapon reloads, the reload circle takes the spot, and once a reload finishes the ammo circle stays hidden until the next shot. With the reload circle off, the spot stays empty during a reload. Both rings have a light shadow, darkest at the bottom left and clear at the top right.
- **Hide on-player health bar** and **Hide on-player recovery bar** (off): hide the bars the game draws over each Helldiver.
- **Damage numbers** (both): numbers in the HUD's own font, with a lighter, closer version of the hard drop shadow of the stats under the player cards in Helldivers Arcade, where damage lands: on enemies the player hits, on players losing health, or both. Off shows none.
- **Damage numbers mode** (accumulated): stacked shows a number for each hit, which starts rising at once, fastest at the start, to make room for the next. Accumulated shows one number for each target that stays in place, adds every hit while it shows, and fades out where it stands; the next hit after it has gone starts a new one.
- **Damage numbers position** (next to the entity) can also put them above the health bar or where each hit landed.

The rows in the pause menu come in English, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, following the game's language, with Obsidian API 1.16.0 or later. The options in Obsidian come in English, Brazilian Portuguese, Chinese, French and Spanish.

Needs Obsidian API 1.16.0 or later for the rows in the pause menu to stay in step with the options in Obsidian. With 1.11.0 to 1.15.x the rows are settings of their own, starting from the options' values. Damage numbers on enemies and enemy bars that follow the player's hits need 1.14.0 or later; with an older API, every damaged enemy gets a bar. Values set in the pause menu before 1.8.0 move into the options the first time 1.8.0 runs.

## For other mods

`obsidian.import("alomare.betterhud").grenade_meter(unit, fraction)` shows a charge meter on the upper grenade icon of the card whose player avatar is `unit`: the icon at 30% opacity underneath, and the full icon over it, cut from the left to `fraction` (0 to 1) of the width. `nil` clears it, and a meter whose unit is gone is dropped. The meter works with the grenade counter on or off.

`obsidian.import("alomare.betterhud").flash_health(unit, seconds)` flashes the health bar of the card whose player avatar is `unit` white, fading out over `seconds` (2 when left out). It shows while the player health bars option is on.

`obsidian.import("alomare.betterhud").status_buildup(unit, kind, fraction, drain, icon)` shows how far a status has built up on an enemy, `"fire"`, `"poison"` or `"stun"`: the icon over the enemy health bar, white with a soft dark outline, filled from the bottom to `fraction` (0 to 1) in the color of the status. It fades in when it first shows. With `drain`, the fill runs down to empty over that many seconds, for a build-up that wears off or an effect running out, and the icon fades out at the end. `icon` is `{ art = material, uv = { u0, v0, u1, v1 } }` for an image a mod ships, or `{ icon = name }` for one of the game's; it is kept from the call before when left out, and a kind that never had one shows as a strip under the bar. `nil` clears it, and the entries of an enemy that is gone are dropped. It shows while the enemy health bars option is on and the enemy has a bar. Icons of several kinds sit side by side.

`obsidian.import("alomare.betterhud").elite_mark(unit, icon)` marks an enemy: its health bar shows from the start, at full health too, and stays while the enemy lives, with `icon` in the middle of the bar, white with the same soft dark outline as the status icons and a little taller than the bar. `icon` takes the same forms as in `status_buildup`. `nil` clears the mark, and the bar then fades as any other; the mark of an enemy that is gone is dropped. Status icons over a marked bar sit clear of the mark. It shows while the enemy health bars option is on.

`obsidian.import("alomare.betterhud").status_effect(unit, kind, seconds, icon)` is the same as `status_buildup(unit, kind, 1, seconds, icon)`: an effect running for `seconds`, the icon full and running down. An effect shows only with an icon. 0 or `nil` seconds ends it.
